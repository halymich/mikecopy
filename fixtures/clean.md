# Rate Ranger watches the fare after you book

You book a refundable hotel room. We check the price every six hours until check-in.

When it drops by more than your threshold, you get an email with the new price and a link to rebook. Last March, one Toronto booking fell from $412 to $289 four days before arrival.

It does not work on non-refundable rates. Those cannot be rebooked, so there is nothing to watch.
